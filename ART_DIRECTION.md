# Legends Unbound — art direction

A single illustrated collection: hand-painted, premium animated fantasy adventure with crisp silhouettes, expressive adult proportions, painterly shading and cinematic rim lighting. Each hero is an original interpretation, never a likeness or adaptation. Backgrounds recede into atmospheric depth; characters stay forward and readable. The frame is antiqued gold and ink, with engraved diamond details and a restrained foil sheen. Palettes: Tarzan—emerald and sunlit gold; Sherlock—smoky blue and amber; Dracula—midnight violet and crimson. All artwork shares a portrait 2:3 coordinate space. Typography and the frame are independent of the artwork.

Image prompts used the shared style above plus the individual character, environment, foreground, and effects directions in the project brief. Core art and transparent overlays were generated individually with the built-in image generation tool. Frame ornament and text are rendered in React Native.

## Generation prompts

All twelve assets used the built-in image generation tool. The shared instruction was: “Premium animated fantasy adventure collectible card illustration, unified high-end hand-painted 2D animation film aesthetic, crisp character outlines and painterly shading, sophisticated expressive proportions, cinematic rim light, richly detailed, no text, no border, no UI, no watermark, not anime, not photorealistic. Portrait 2:3 composition.”

- Tarzan character: Original heroic athletic jungle adventurer, long dark hair, intelligent face, dynamic three-quarter leap reaching toward the viewer, practical original jungle clothing, green and gold rim lighting. Isolated on transparency.
- Sherlock character: Original charismatic Victorian detective, sharp eyes, tailored dark coat and windblown scarf, three-quarter pose with magnifying glass toward the viewer, cool blue shadow and warm amber rim light. Isolated on transparency.
- Dracula character: Original elegant vampire aristocrat, pale expressive face, dark hair, dramatic black and crimson cape, three-quarter pose with hand reaching toward the viewer, violet and crimson rim light. Isolated on transparency.
- Environments: An emerald jungle with giant trees, waterfall and sun shafts; foggy Victorian London rooftops with warm gas lamps; and a moonlit gothic castle with mountains and storm clouds. No characters.
- Foregrounds: Transparent edge-framing jungle leaves and vines; windblown Victorian papers and smoke; and bats with black-crimson ribbons, respectively.
- Effects: Transparent sparse gold and green motes; amber dust and blue mist; and crimson sparks with violet mist, respectively.

- Shared frame: A transparent 2:3 antiqued gold and dark bronze border with fine engraved corners and a crest; empty transparent interior, no characters or typography. The frame is a reusable PNG with native gem, title, and plaque components layered separately.

## Character title wordmarks

The separate transparent title images were generated with the built-in image generation tool. Their common prompt specified original, legible, single-line lettering with shallow dimensional extrusion, edge highlights, no character or scenery, and exact spelling. Tarzan drew on early jungle adventure newspaper strips and pulp covers; Sherlock Holmes on Victorian illustrated detective publishing; Dracula on classic gothic horror comic covers. These are interpretive references, not reproductions of existing logos. The app moves each wordmark independently with the card's tilt.



## Shared frame revision

The built-in image generation tool produced one transparent shared frame: neutral antique-gold and bronze, geometric bevels, rounded corners, and a clear center, without character-specific motifs. The same PNG is used for all three cards, above the environment and behind the character.

## Prototype card back

The shared reverse was generated with the built-in image generation tool: a portrait, text-free Victorian detective card back with a magnifying glass, clues, brass engraving, and a midnight-blue palette. It is used as a shared Sherlock-inspired prototype back for all three cards.

Back artwork prompt: “Create an original back face design for a premium collectible playing card in portrait 2:3. Sherlock Holmes inspired Victorian detective mystery, but no character portrait, no actor, no copyrighted logos, no words or lettering. An elegant symmetrical deep midnight blue and antique brass engraved design: a subtle central magnifying glass motif, intricate Victorian geometric guilloché, tiny clue-like ornamental details and ornate corner flourishes. Hand-painted premium animated adventure style, crafted brass and ink textures, controlled highlights, sophisticated and legible at phone size. The artwork fills the entire canvas to the edges with a neatly inset border and rounded-card visual. No mockup, no perspective, no background outside the card, no card face stats, no watermark.”
