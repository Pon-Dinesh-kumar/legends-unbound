# Legends Unbound project memory

For a name-only card request, follow [the card generation pipeline](docs/CARD_GENERATION_PIPELINE.md), [art direction](ART_DIRECTION.md), [asset guidelines](docs/CARD_ASSET_GUIDELINES.md), and [Jane Porter lessons](docs/JANE_PORTER_LESSONS.md). These are repository instructions for future card work, not a claim that the pipeline is automated.

- Research the named character's original public-domain source and document the version and jurisdiction. Choose their own source-appropriate iconic pose, not a default reaching hand. Preserve the established collection art style; each character needs their own distinctive title and newly generated scene, foreground, effect and particle art.
- Before generating anything, record the complete composition, layer/occlusion plan and exactly three distinct, character-specific Unbound animation tracks. Seek variety across characters in both ideas and techniques. Consider clean hair movement for female characters when appropriate, without forcing it. Generate as many registered assets as the design needs; optimize and measure the runtime result.
- Make each track clearly noticeable at real phone card size while remaining slow, smooth and natural. Check full loops and transitions for seams, cuts, duplicate pixels, ghosting, detached anchors, clipping and underpaint gaps; an imperceptible or defective track fails. Never claim an unfinished card is complete.
- Use the same art in all levels: Origin inside the frame and static, Awakened top/side character frame break and static, Unbound frame break plus parallax and three card-specific animations. Keep the bottom quarter behind the frame and all environment inside. Preserve reduced motion, preloading and lifecycle behavior.
- Rarity is expressed by the shared border and its integrated gems, not by per-character art. Existing cards use Legendary gold except Jane, who uses the clean Epic purple frame and purple gems. Common and Rare designs are undecided. Do not change Legendary visuals when adjusting Epic.
- Verify the actual web rendering at desktop and phone sizes, including all levels, frame, title, stats, flip, selection and loading. Physical Android sensor/performance validation and the previously reported crash and horizontal line remain pending user ADB setup. Do not build an APK unless requested.
- Treat performance as part of every change: avoid duplicate decoding, unnecessary updates and inactive rendering, and profile representative interactions. Preserve the requested visual quality, preload and transitions; report measurements and untested devices honestly.
