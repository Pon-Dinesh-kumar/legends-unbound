## Identity and design

Jane Porter appears in Edgar Rice Burroughs's *Tarzan of the Apes*, first published in 1912 (book edition 1914). The [original text](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/78/pg78-images.html) identifies her Baltimore home and her father Professor Porter. The original novel is public domain in the United States; see [Duke's public domain reference](https://web.law.duke.edu/cspd/publicdomainday/2026/). Research accessed 2026-11-20. Public-domain status is jurisdiction-specific; this is not a clearance of later adaptations or trademarks. Avoid adaptation-specific costume, likenesses and logos.

Card ID `jane`, Epic; proposed cost 3, power 5. A resourceful period adventurer in a cream explorer blouse, earth-toned skirt and practical leather, with long blonde hair, an original warm-colored scarf and a brass compass. These wardrobe and compass choices are original art direction, not claims about the book. African jungle background; warm cinematic light; match the painterly 2.5D animated-feature finish of the existing cards. Give her an original custom `JANE PORTER` title. Keep her face readable and within Origin, with only her upper silhouette breaking the frame for Awakened and Unbound.

## Three planned tracks (before generation)

Use a common 1024 × 1536 source coordinate system and retain exactly the same resting artwork across all evolution levels. Register exact contours and anchors only after inspecting the generated source. Origin and Awakened show all three resting details; selected Unbound animates them independently. Reduced motion, back face, app background and selection stop the animation. No whole-picture ambient motion.

1. **Loose hair lock:** a large, isolated outer lock against transparent space, attached to the back of her head, behind the face. The root remains fixed; slow weighted bend toward the tip over roughly 7 seconds. Create finished underpainting if any pixels are exposed. Reject detached roots, visible cuts, moving face, or rigidity.
2. **Scarf tail:** an independent loose fabric tail over transparent space attached at the neck, behind her hand and face. Its root remains fixed; a two-joint bend over roughly 6 seconds, out of phase with the hair. Finish underlying artwork if covered, and reject seams or fluttering as a rigid plate.
3. **Compass glass reflection:** a moving narrow reflection travels across the glass of the compass held by Jane, clipped strictly inside its painted rim and parented to the character. Approximately 5 seconds with smooth entry/exit, with the compass and hand fixed. The compass and its underlying reflections remain visible in every level. Reject an opacity-only flash or reflection outside the lens.

## Layer and integration plan

Back-to-front: opaque jungle background; clipped ambient foreground/effects; character with hair/scarf rest pieces, finished base and local compass reflection; universal rarity frame with integrated cost/power and three evolution sockets; character top/side frame break; title and stats. Follow the established renderer's actual depth ordering and lower-quarter clip. The Epic frame is shared across Epic characters: identical layout and badge positions to the existing Legendary frame, plain, saturated purple metal with no frame sparkles or light sweeps; its purple gems retain their shine. Legendary cards keep their approved gold frame. Common and Rare are named but their border art remains undecided. Rarity changes the border only; evolution never changes the art or border.

Assets: generated character and jungle, transparent title, and shared Epic frame; pre-existing shared back and rarity-specific gems; separately registered hair/scarf material or anchored rigs and finished underpaint when necessary. Preload every source and required moving layer before reveal; preserve load errors/retry. Add Jane to registry, make the collection count and selector work for four and fix Sherlock's placeholder rarity. Use existing tilt, flip, clipping, parallax and lifecycle policies.

## Acceptance

Inspect each separated rest composite and loop boundary at 200–400% and phone card size. Reject ghosts, cuts, lost coverage, title errors, badge misalignment, environment frame breaks and face clipping. Compare all three levels, tilt, switch, flip, loading and reduced motion in browser, and run TypeScript checks. A physical Android and iOS check remains required for performance and sensors; do not claim device verification without hardware. If any of the three tracks fails, leave this card explicitly incomplete rather than counting a bad or invisible effect.

No jungle, no frame, no vignette, no text, no logos, no letters, no watermark, no checkerboard. Original look, do not imitate any animated film adaptation.”
