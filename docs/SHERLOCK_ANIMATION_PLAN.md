# Sherlock animation plan

Sherlock keeps one shared 1024 × 1536 illustration and the existing scarf rest appearance at every evolution. A complete card requires exactly three independently planned and reviewed card-specific tracks; shared frame, title and background effects do not count. Origin and Awakened remain static; only the selected Unbound front may animate. The upper and lower clipped copies must share the same character transform and clock.

1. **Scarf: existing prototype.** Three registered poses over a finished character base, attached in front of the coat with a feathered root. Keep the body and face fixed; inspect pose transitions, attachment and frame-break clipping. Physical-device performance and quality are not yet verified.
2. **Magnifying-glass reflection: current prototype.** The lens and its painted stationary reflections remain in the same place in every evolution. A slow moving optical reflection is confined to the lens interior, behind its rim, in the existing character coordinate system. It moves spatially across the glass; it is not a full-character shimmer or an opacity-only flash. Its clipped rest frame matches the unchanged illustration, and it follows the held glass/hand, parallax and both character clips. Stop it when the card is inactive, flipped, backgrounded, or reduced motion is requested. Reject light spilling onto face, hand, rim, scarf or background; visible hard cuts, unrealistic glaring stripes, a shifted glass, or imperceptible movement at phone size.
3. **Coat hem: candidate only, not implemented.** Review a specific outward hem or coat fold for an anchored, separated rest pose and finished underpaint. Keep the body and hand still, plan depth against the scarf, and use authored in-betweens or a properly weighted rig if a rigid transform cannot look natural. Reject gaps, duplicated fabric, detached edges or motion hidden by the title. Do not count a failed candidate as complete.

The lens uses a code-drawn localized highlight and the existing artwork, so it adds no raster assets. Before claiming completion, inspect all loop poses, transitions and tilt extremes at actual phone card size; verify both evolution levels, frame break, flips, loading, card switching, app foreground/resume, reduced motion, and performance on representative Android and iOS devices. Sherlock remains incomplete until the third track and all applicable quality gates pass.
