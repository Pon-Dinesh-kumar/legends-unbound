# Tarzan animation plan

Tarzan's existing shared 1024 × 1536 background and character are the rest artwork for all levels. This card remains incomplete until three independent tracks pass the collection quality gate. No alternate evolution art is generated.

1. **Distant waterfall (prototype in scope):** attach to the background coordinate system, behind Tarzan and foreground foliage. The still waterfall at approximately x=770–890, y=310–690 is the shared rest pose. Test two offset, continuously descending samples of the existing background with constant coverage at their handoffs, inside separately feathered masks over the main cascade and the lower falls; use a smaller displacement for the shorter drops so their sources stay anchored; retain the original background beneath it. The mask and waterfall follow the background's cover fit and parallax. Pause on other evolutions, inactive card, back, backgrounded app, and reduced motion. Reject any noticeable double edges, stationary-landscape movement, seams, looping pops, or imperceptible motion at phone size; revise or leave the track incomplete.
2. **Loose loincloth tail (planned, not implemented):** separate a connected lower cloth flap, with finished underpainting beneath it and unchanged rest silhouette. Anchor at the belt, behind hands and relevant body portions; use several registered deforming poses with natural weight. Reject exposed holes, duplicated fabric, edge seams, or body movement. Requires an asset-separation and occlusion feasibility review.
3. **Hair lock (planned, not implemented):** separate a small outward lock without altering the face or hairline; finish the underlying hair and anchor the moving root. Author a slow irregular loop distinct from the cloth. Reject floating locks, gaps, flicker, changes to the face, or silhouette pops. Requires an asset-separation feasibility review.

These are plans, not claims of finished animation. Verify the waterfall across its loop, both ends, phone-sized web, clipping and foreground occlusion, drag, sensor-driven parallax, flip, evolution changes, selection, reduced motion, loading, app lifecycle and actual device performance when hardware is available. No new image assets are needed for the waterfall prototype; reuse the preloaded background. The other two plans are intentionally not implemented by this request.
