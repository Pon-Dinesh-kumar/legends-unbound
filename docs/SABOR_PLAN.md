## Source and brief

- Requested identity: Sabor from the Tarzan universe. Source version: Edgar Rice Burroughs, *Tarzan of the Apes*, the 1914 book edition derived from the 1912 magazine story. The [original book scan](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bc/Tarzan_of_the_apes_%28IA_tarzanoftheapes00burrrich%29.pdf) and [chapter XI text](https://teachers.americanliterature.com/author/edgar-rice-burroughs/book/tarzan-of-the-apes/chapter-xi-king-of-the-apes) describe Sabor as a lioness. The [public-domain early newspaper adaptation](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/bf/Tarzan_first_comic_strips_-_Tarzan_of_The_Apes_and_The_Return_of_Tarzan.pdf) also depicts her as a lioness. The word is used as an ape-language name/type, not evidence of one immutable individual across adaptations. Base the card on the book-edition lioness, not a later film's leopard design.
- U.S. public-domain baseline: the 1914 book predates works entering the U.S. public domain from 1930 in 2026; see [Duke Law](https://cspd.law.duke.edu/publicdomainday/2026). Research accessed 2026-11-20. Rights vary by jurisdiction; do not infer clearance for later adaptations or trademarks.
- ID `sabor`, display name `SABOR`, Rare, proposed cost 3 / power 5. An alert, powerful tawny lioness stalking or crouching in a natural predator pose, not a person reaching toward the viewer. Recognizable silhouette, focused face, natural anatomy; no modern adaptation likeness. Thick layered painterly fur and warm light in the collection's 2.5D animated-feature style. Unique one-line clawed stone-and-ochre title art.
- Setting: an original jungle riverbank clearing at golden hour, with unique layered foliage, stone and haze, not Tarzan's or Jane's layers. Arrange the head and tail for top/side character frame break; keep the background and lower quarter inside the border. One shared composition for all levels.
- Rarity: one reusable simpler orange/yellow Rare frame with integrated blue/red stat shields and three sockets, no animated border or marker effects at any evolution. It preserves the same card layout and content quality as every other rarity; actual character animation remains Unbound-only.

## Exactly three independently planned animations

All three are local, distinct and must be visibly present in their rest pose in Origin, Awakened and Unbound. Their parents, depths and masks must follow the relevant character or environment transform. Only the selected foreground Unbound face animates; stop on flip, selection change, background and reduced motion. Evaluate at phone size and full-loop extremes, including tilt and frame clipping. Do not count an invisible or defective track.

1. **Tail tuft and flexible tail:** the lioness's rear tail exits the body into transparent space, with an independently isolated flexible tuft. Parent to Sabor behind the body; root attached to the hindquarters, with body and face fixed. Plan a separate rest tail/tuft and a finished plausible body beneath overlap. Prefer a weighted two-or-more-joint bend or registered in-betweens, roughly 6–8 seconds, not a rigid cutout swing. Generate a clear exposed silhouette to permit separation. Determine exact source-coordinate anchors and safe displacement from the selected art, then reject detached roots, cuts, double contours, image gaps and jitter. If not feasible, revise art/technique or leave incomplete.
2. **Ear flick:** one visible outer ear, naturally attached behind the forehead/face. Isolate the outer ear, complete fur beneath, and use registered drawings or a small anchored deformation; a distinct occasional smooth turn and return, roughly 9–12 seconds, with a rest interval. Keep the face, eye, head and other ear fixed; avoid any appearance of a duplicate ear, unnatural rotation or flickering seam. Exact anchor and overlap must be recorded from selected art; reject if there is no clean underpaint or motion is not visible at phone size.
3. **Original upper-left sunlight (revised again at user request):** remove the separately drawn, conflicting shafts. Restrict a soft rotating illumination to the bright pixels of the existing background around its upper-left sunset at approximately (250,345), using the original source as a luminance mask and its existing background transform. The sun and scene remain the original artwork; no second ray source or broad shafts may appear elsewhere. Keep the rest angle identical on every level and rotate only on the selected Unbound front. Keep the effect behind the lioness, clip inside the art window, and leave the fern still. Reject hard mask edges, detached or conflicting rays, dark-region spill, zoom, an evolution pop, or motion that cannot be seen at phone size. Test loop turning points and reduced motion; revise or leave incomplete if quality fails.

- `assets/cards/sabor/ear-underpaint.png` is a generated ear-removal candidate. It changed pixels outside the ear, so **the full candidate is never used as the base**. The prototype clips its contribution to the ear patch and blends it out at the attachment; the original character remains the base elsewhere. The ear waits at rest, smoothly flicks out and back, then rests again; its 10.5-second cycle avoids continuously changing the SVG during the long idle interval.
- A generated tail-removal candidate changed the body silhouette and was not selected or bundled. The tail prototype instead uses the original transparent-space tail pixels with root overlap; the body remains the original. Its upper free portion pivots near (150,405); a softly overlapped tuft bends near (240,155). The ear pivots near (560,330) and rests between flicks. These source coordinates are artwork-specific. The original moving-fern prototype has been superseded at the user's request; its generated artwork remains a static foreground. `SaborSunrays` uses the existing background as a luminance mask to constrain a soft moving highlight to the upper-left sunlight, sharing the background transform and resting angle across all levels; only Unbound slowly rotates it back and forth. The earlier separate long shafts were removed after the user identified conflicting rays. The character rig falls back to the original still image until its SVG parts load.
- The upper character and foreground rigs run only on the selected foreground Unbound front and stop on back, selection changes, reduced motion and app background. The lower clipped character copy stays still because the tail and ear do not enter the bottom-quarter clip. Reassess this if the art, motion range or clipping changes. `ear-underpaint.png` is included in the preload and mounted-layer readiness list. Repeated full-canvas SVG references and masks can still be expensive; measure on physical devices.
