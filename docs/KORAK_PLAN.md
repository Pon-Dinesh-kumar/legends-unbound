# Korak — Epic card production plan

## Source and identity

Korak is Jack Clayton, the son of Tarzan and Jane, in Edgar Rice Burroughs's *The Son of Tarzan* (1915 serial, 1917 book). The [Project Gutenberg text](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/90), consulted 21 November 2026, identifies this version as public domain in the United States; verify rights and trademarks separately for other jurisdictions. The text describes his jungle life, rope, knife and spear. Base the card on this early novel, not later comic or screen designs. Keep this an original interpretation; do not imply a particular scene or costume is a verbatim illustration. ID `korak`, displayed KORAK, Epic. Proposed cost 3, power 5, subject to gameplay design.

## Story, pose and lighting

An adult Korak is paused high in the jungle canopy after rain, balanced in a low side-on crouch on a thick branch and looking toward something below, a spear held close and a grass rope at his side. This attentive hunter's pause is a story-appropriate interpretation, not a repetition of Tarzan's forward reach or La's standing pose. Dark storm-blue/green canopy, wet bark and muted reflected moonlight create a distinct nighttime setting; no sun shafts, golden opening or borrowed jungle art. Use the collection's painterly animated-feature finish. Clear facial silhouette and hands, do not crowd the stat shields with the spear, rope, head or frame break. Keep enough setting visible and the large title and bottom rail readable. The shared Epic purple border and gem art are the only rarity difference; same underlying art and rest objects across all levels. Origin clips inside; Awakened unlocks character-only top/side break; Unbound adds parallax and the three unique tracks. Lower quarter stays behind the frame.

## Animation-first feasibility gate: three independent studies

These must be generated, assembled and tested at intended card size before final base approval. All moving elements appear at the same rest locations on all levels; only selected active Unbound animates. Never count a changing value, global drift or an imperceptible track as a pass. At each failure, revise and retest rather than retrofitting a static final image.

| Track | Character/story rationale and alternative | Required independent assets and technique | Acceptance and fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grass-rope coil | Korak carries a rope in the novel. A suspended coil gives his jungle toolkit a readable identity. Prefer a weighted joint with a fixed hand attachment over a whole-character pivot. | Separate natural loop/rope on alpha; complete hand, branch and background behind its whole movement range; registered attachment and foreground fingers if overlap requires them. Design it beside the character, not over an existing finished hand. | Observe a believable slow swing at phone size without the rope detaching, crossing a limb incorrectly, cutting, flickering or doubling. If it cannot be attached convincingly, revise pose/rig or replace the track before final base. |
| Canopy rain | A localized rain curtain communicates the storm-lit hunt without adding a generic light effect. Compare a seamless scrolling transparent rain strip with registered sprite frames. | Seamless transparent streak field in a bounded background opening, fixed local clip/soft feather, uninterrupted repeat and finished background beneath. Keep scene and head free from unwanted streaks. | Slow consistent diagonal fall with constant coverage and no visible seam, rectangular edge, popping, or movement outside frame; same static rain trace on lower levels. |
| Cloud lightning (user revision) | A branching storm channel in the moonlit sky expresses the active storm. Compare registered authored frames and masked local illumination; do not place a new unrelated bolt on the character or move the whole sky. | A separate, transparent sequence with a fixed origin and common path, a complete sky behind it, and a faint resting channel in every level. Animate the path and localized light only in Unbound. | Clearly recognizable but not harsh or distracting; same attachment and rest position, no random teleportation, rectangular glow, abrupt cutoff or effect outside the frame. |

Pre-base table for each: story/action; alternative and selected method; source dimensions, mask/alpha and anchor; parent/occlusion and hidden paint; actual asset paths/preload; frames or clip/cycle; screenshot and motion evidence at phone size; pass/fail/unverified and next revision. No final base approval until the three feasible motion-ready studies pass. Prefer visual quality and natural attachment over a track count.

## Layer, integration and review plan

Use 1024×1536 registered portrait art: original opaque moonlit canopy and river depth; rain and lightning confined to a suitable opening; branch with painted hidden surfaces; transparent Korak and spear with completed rope-attachment area and any necessary finger occlusion; independently mounted rope; original foreground/effect art; unique dimensional KORAK wordmark; shared Epic frame, stats, gems and back. Record source coordinates after studies. Fit the environment to his feet, including changing its size or geometry as needed, while keeping the same rest crop across levels. Plan overscan for tilt, keep landscape inside the frame, and keep all three rest details identical across levels. Preload every selected layer, handle failures, and stop motion on switch, flip, app background and reduced motion. Review Origin, Awakened and Unbound at 320/390 px and desktop; check neutral and drag extremes, stat clearance, motion loop boundaries and interaction, loading, reduced motion and flip. Record real-time evidence and strict player-perspective review. Android sensors, memory and performance remain unverified until a device is connected; do not build an APK unless requested.

## Status

Motion studies were produced before final base approval with the built-in image generation tool. [Rope](../assets/cards/korak/studies/rope.png), [bright rain](../assets/cards/korak/studies/rain-bright.png), and [drip frames](../assets/cards/korak/studies/drip-sheet.png) are separately painted. The first [rain candidate](../assets/cards/korak/studies/rain.png) appeared dark in the browser and its first/last rows differed, so it was rejected; overlapping feathered passes are being tested for the replacement rather than assuming seamless texture edges. A baked branch droplet was removed from the [canopy study](../assets/cards/korak/studies/canopy-clean.png) before adding the separate drip. The [character gesture study](../assets/cards/korak/studies/character-study.png) and canopy were assembled with these three studies in [the browser motion-study page](korak-motion-study.html). The original branch tip was hidden by the foot, so the test drip was moved to an exposed branch underside; the rope's attachment and the drip's exact contact need further inspection in the actual card. Generated scene and character remain provisional. Full-loop visual evidence, mobile performance and physical device checks have not passed; do not treat changing transforms as quality approval.

**User correction:** the existing branch does not convincingly support Korak's feet. Revise the scene's scale or branch geometry to make contact, improve the rain, and replace the drip with a fixed-origin storm lightning effect. The previous drip and rain assets are studies, not accepted final tracks.

**Revised preview:** a [new branch study](../assets/cards/korak/studies/canopy-branch-v2.png) slopes beneath the feet, and the character is fitted separately for Origin and the frame-breaking levels while the scene's rest crop remains consistent. Browser stills at 390 px show contact in Origin and Awakened; assess the full motion and tilt on a physical device. The [lightning asset](../assets/cards/korak/studies/lightning.png) replaces the drip: a faint fixed channel stays in all levels, and Unbound reveals a brighter copy along that same path before fading. An earlier generated lightning sheet looked like a glowing patch and was rejected. The first fine-rain revision was too faint; the preview uses smaller repeated copies of the brighter texture with overlapped motion and feathered boundaries. Inspect rain tile joins and timing in motion before quality approval. Browser behavior checks and native/device profiling are recorded separately; a screenshot or changing values alone cannot certify smoothness, visual quality or performance.

**User feedback:** the user praised Korak's variation in color, pose and creativity. They clarified that sunlight is allowed when the story suits it and asked for open-ended, character-specific future animation ideas and techniques. This feedback accepts the creative direction; it does not certify physical-device performance or every visual quality gate.

**Rain handoff correction:** the previous lower rain pass stayed visible while wrapping; the upper pass was transparent and could not hide that reset. Both passes now use complementary `sin²`/`cos²` opacity so each wraps only when its own contribution is zero. Browser screenshots sampled immediately on both sides of the loop boundary and midpoint handoff; the largest channel difference was 2 and 3 levels respectively in the captured samples. This check does not establish that all underlying tile joins or physical-device motion are perfect.
