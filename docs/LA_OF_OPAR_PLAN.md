# La of Opar — Rare card production plan

## Source and identity

La is the high priestess of Opar and a devotee of the sun in Edgar Rice Burroughs's *The Return of Tarzan* (first published 1913). The [Project Gutenberg edition](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/81), consulted 20 November 2026, identifies the text as public domain in the United States. The novel places her in Opar's temple with a ceremonial knife; the later early [*Tarzan and the Jewels of Opar*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/92) also treats the knife as an insignia of her office. The headdress, costume and sun mechanism below are original creative interpretation, not claims about specific textual descriptions. Avoid later screen/comic designs. Rights and trademark status outside the US remain unverified. Card ID `la`; display title **LA OF OPAR**; Rare; proposed gameplay values cost 4, power 5, subject to game design.

## Distinctive composition and layers

Subject: commanding adult priestess in a poised three-quarter stance with an inward-held ceremonial knife, no reaching toward camera. Her strong profile, headdress and raised knife silhouette provide a natural top/side frame break without encroaching on the blue/red stat shields. Center face within upper middle, leave the temple legible. She wears original layered ceremonial garments, bronze and dark turquoise with restrained ember accents; no copied adaptation costume. Match the established painterly animated-feature style. Use a new Opar stone sanctuary at twilight, warm firelight against deep teal stone, a stone brazier, and a sun-wheel relief behind her; no borrowed jungle layers. Knife, hands and face must remain legible above the bottom title. Use the shared static amber Rare border and shared back. Character and environment remain the same in all three evolutions; Origin clipped, Awakened top/side character break, Unbound adds depth and three motions. Keep lower character behind frame and scene clipped; preserve all-level rest crops.

Canvas and stacking: 1024×1536 registered portrait; opaque completed sanctuary background and static wheel housing; separately painted wheel centered in its circular housing behind the character; independent stationary brazier body behind/aside the character with separate flame and complete dark interior underpaint; character base with finished shoulder/garment underpaint behind independent hanging ceremonial ornament; separate hanging ornament with a defined attachment point; foreground stone/embers as needed; large unique transparent LA OF OPAR lettering; shared frame, stats and gems. Retain safe bleed for parallax and avoid effects appearing only at Unbound.

## Required three-track pre-base feasibility gate

These studies are generated and evaluated before final base-card art. Record actual paths, loop demonstrations, registration and pass/fail; a description or a changing transform alone is not evidence of quality. The creative goal is the life of Opar's temple and La's priestly authority, not a generic scarf/glint recipe.

| Track | Distinct purpose and chosen technique | Asset and registration study | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Sacred brazier flame | Local organically changing fire silhouette, using independently authored registered poses or a local flow mask, anchored at brazier bowl; vary tongue shapes, not a blinking opacity overlay. | Transparent flame poses, finished bowl/interior and feathered local clip, fixed foot and complete underpaint; compare against masked UV/noise and choose what works in the renderer. | Clearly readable at phone size, slow smooth uninterrupted shape change and loop, no detached or rectangular edges; still flame visible in lower levels. |
| 2. Sun-wheel mechanism | A slow turning engraved sun-wheel visible behind La, communicating Opar's sun worship; rigid rotation is appropriate for a mechanical disc. | Isolated round wheel and independently finished circular stone housing, exact common center/pivot, sufficient radial clearance and no seams; compare full rotation with rocking mechanism. | Wheel rotation is identifiable, continuous through 360°, remains behind character, no colliding with badges or showing an empty housing, identical resting position across levels. |
| 3. Ceremonial pendant | A short weighted metal pendant suspended from La's chest or belt, moving with a small natural swing without rotating her body. | Independently painted pendant with a fixed loop at its upper end, separate complete underlying cloth, chain attachment/occlusion and neutral alignment. Compare anchored rig with authored poses. | Distinct, visible independent swing, anchored loop and no cut, ghost, or duplicate underneath; rest object in every level. |

Each track's study paths, renderer behavior, actual visual observations, fallback and final package links must be recorded here before final base art is approved. Generate registered art or masks as needed without an arbitrary image limit. Reject the study if the motion is cut, too subtle, generic, misaligned or hidden; improve and retest rather than counting it. The studies are integrated in the preview; full-loop phone-size review and physical-device checks remain unverified.

### Pre-base studies and findings

- Built-in image generation produced [the flame rest](../assets/cards/la/studies/flame-rest.png), [wheel](../assets/cards/la/studies/sun-wheel.png), and [pendant](../assets/cards/la/studies/pendant.png), plus two alternate flame poses. The images are individually transparent; they are preliminary studies, not registered final card assets.
- The first flame experiment cross-faded separately generated poses and produced visibly doubled tips; rejected. A second animated vector clip produced a hard outline and detached pixels; rejected. An eight-frame painted [flame sheet](../assets/cards/la/studies/flame-sheet.png) was then generated; the eight alpha>100 base bounds match to approximately one source pixel, with frame 8 and frame 1 showing similar tip positions. The browser [motion study](la-motion-study.html) displays this sheet without crossfade, a rotating wheel and an anchored pendant on provisional shapes. Frames were inspected at 220 px study width and source registration measured. Full-loop player review, exact placement in finished art, underpaint and native performance are still unverified; no final gate is passed yet.
- The wheel and pendant were placed over the generated scene and character, respectively, and the pendant has a painted clothing surface beneath it. The circular housing, pendant attachment and their motion must still be judged through full loops in the actual composition; isolation and stills alone do not certify that they remain convincing.
- After user feedback, the pendant was reduced and anchored to the collar so its lower edge stays clear of La's forearm. The neutral position and six-degree swing extreme were inspected in browser screenshots. The existing brazier and flame position was retained at the user's direction. These stills do not certify a full loop or physical-device behavior.
- The user said this card felt fresh and appreciated its innovative animation-layer design. This is positive feedback on the creative direction, not proof of full-loop or physical-device certification. For future cards, retain the research-and-study process but choose new pose, light sources and three motions specific to the next character; do not repeat La's combination as a template.

## Integration, performance and review plan

After the three pre-base studies pass, generate one coherent base composition and all separate layers in the exact validated registration; implement inside the current card compositor, shared Rare frame and asset-preload/readiness graph. Stop inactive motion on card switch, flip, app background, unmount and reduced motion. Verify all three levels at 320/390 px and desktop, rest and tilt extremes, actual frame break and stat/title clearance, all three isolated/full loops and handoffs, lower border and gem placement, loading, retry, selection, flip and reduced motion. Profile browser interaction and memory without reducing quality. Record screenshot and moving evidence, and use the strict player-perspective review. Physical Android sensor, performance and masking validation remain pending the user's device setup; no APK unless requested. This plan is not a completed card or proof that any study passes.

**Current preview evidence:** La is registered using the shared Rare border, with the wheel and painted flame behind the character and the pendant attached to the character layer. Browser previews covered three levels at 390 px, and parallax overscan checks covered both drag extremes at 320 and 390 px. Automated browser checks observed all three tracks changing in Unbound, resting in Origin, stopping on flip and respecting reduced motion; TypeScript passed. These checks do not establish visual quality throughout full loops, device performance, or the remaining acceptance criteria above.
